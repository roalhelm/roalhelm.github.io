My iPhone app has been submitted to Apple for internal review

I have now officially submitted my iPhone app to Apple for internal review. This is an exciting moment because it means the project has moved beyond development and into the real-world review process.

For me, that means one thing above all: the app is no longer just a private project, but something that is now being evaluated under Apple’s review conditions. I am curious to see whether Apple will flag anything related to design, functionality, privacy, or App Review Guidelines.

What makes this stage especially interesting is the mix of preparation and uncertainty. You have tested, refined, and improved the app as much as possible, and then the remaining step is simply to wait. The key questions become: what will Apple notice, what is critical, and when will Apple respond?

I am looking forward to the feedback and eager to see how the review process unfolds. Often, the most valuable lessons are not the obvious bugs, but the small details that only become visible under real review.

This is the moment where a project shifts from “works for me” to “ready for real use”. I am excited to see what comes back and to learn from the process.

Now it is a matter of waiting, processing the feedback, and planning the next steps. I am very curious to see what comes out of it.
