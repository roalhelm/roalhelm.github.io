Curiosity-driven development.

LetMeTryThis.dev is my place for small software projects, scripts, app ideas, AI experiments, and tools around endpoint management and all the other problems you deal with.

Hi, I'm Ronny

Endpoint management enthusiast, PowerShell nerd, and passionate “let’s see if this works” kind of person.
