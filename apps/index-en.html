LeckerBierchen

LeckerBierchen supports day-to-day service work: create tables and guests, record orders, use quick checkout for walk-in customers, and settle open amounts clearly.

The app is intentionally simple, runs locally on the device, and focuses on privacy, stability, and fast handling during busy shifts.
